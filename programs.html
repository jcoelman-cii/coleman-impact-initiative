Our Programs

Building stability, opportunity, and environmental resilience across Humboldt County.

Veterans Stability & Safety Program

A hybrid remote and in-person support model for veterans navigating housing instability, alcohol & drug recovery, personal safety, and long-term stability. This program leverages community partnerships and trauma-informed care to deliver meaningful, accessible support.

Learn More

Rural Recycling Access (RIG6)

An 18-site automated Reverse Vending Machine network serving tribal nations, rural communities, high schools, and healthcare institutions. This project expands CRV redemption access, reduces landfill flow, and strengthens environmental justice outcomes across the region.

Learn More

Community Outreach & Education

Structured recycling education, environmental stewardship curriculum, and community engagement initiatives delivered through school districts, tribal partners, and healthcare institutions.
