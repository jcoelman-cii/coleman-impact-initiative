About Coleman Impact Initiative

Infrastructure-driven solutions for rural, tribal, and underserved communities.

Who We Are

Coleman Impact Initiative (CII) is a California-registered nonprofit organization headquartered in Eureka, CA. We operate at the intersection of environmental justice, tribal sovereignty, and community economic development, delivering equitable, infrastructure-focused solutions to communities historically excluded from California’s recycling and resource access systems.

Our Mission

Our mission is to expand access, opportunity, and stability for rural and tribal communities across Humboldt County and neighboring regions. We work collaboratively with tribal nations, school districts, healthcare institutions, and community partners to build sustainable systems that improve environmental outcomes and strengthen community resilience.

Our Approach

CII’s model emphasizes long-term infrastructure investment, government-to-government partnership, and community-led program design. We prioritize clean transportation, off-grid energy solutions, equitable access to recycling systems, and workforce development opportunities that uplift local residents.
