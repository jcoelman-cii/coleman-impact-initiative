Future Initiatives

Long-term infrastructure visions designed to strengthen Humboldt County and support tribal sovereignty.

Samoa Peninsula Clean Energy & Materials Campus

CII is exploring a long-term vision for a publicly held, tribally co-governed clean-energy and materials campus on the Samoa Peninsula. This concept integrates renewable generation, advanced energy storage, hydrogen production, and clean materials recovery into a single community-owned utility model.

The campus would leverage offshore wind, tidal energy, concentrated solar, and emerging storage technologies to create a resilient, ethical, and community-centered energy backbone for Humboldt County.

Ocean Plastics Recovery & Depolymerization

CII is evaluating technologies that convert ocean plastics into usable materials through pyrolysis, depolymerization, and advanced recovery processes. Recovered materials may be repurposed into semi-dense recycled blocks suitable for gravity-based energy storage systems.

This initiative aligns with statewide and federal priorities for marine debris removal, environmental justice, and circular economy development.

Hydrogen Production & Clean Industrial Processes

Future development may include green hydrogen production using desalinated seawater and renewable power. Hydrogen could support clean transportation, industrial processes, and long-duration energy storage.

Publicly Held Utility Model

CII’s long-term vision includes the formation of a community-owned public utility that reinvests all surplus revenue into local communities, tribal partners, and regional infrastructure. This model ensures that no private investors extract wealth from essential services and that governance remains transparent, ethical, and community-driven.
