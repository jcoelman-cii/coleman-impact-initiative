Governance & Transparency

Our commitment to ethical stewardship, community ownership, and public accountability.

Our Values

Coleman Impact Initiative operates with a strict commitment to public benefit, environmental justice, tribal sovereignty, and community-centered governance. We reject extractive economic models and ensure that all programs and future initiatives serve the public good.

Organizational Structure

CII is a California-registered nonprofit public benefit corporation. Governance is provided by a Board of Directors committed to transparency, ethical oversight, and long-term community resilience.

Public Utility Principles

  • No private investor enrichment.
  • Community and tribal co-governance.
  • Surplus revenue reinvested locally.
  • Transparent decision-making processes.
  • Anti-extraction and anti-speculation commitments.
  • Long-term stewardship over short-term profit.

Why Bylaws Are Not Posted Publicly

While CII is committed to transparency, full bylaws are legal documents that can be misinterpreted or misused when posted publicly. Instead, we provide clear summaries of our governance principles, values, and commitments to ensure the public understands how we operate.

Full bylaws are available upon request to partners, agencies, and stakeholders.
