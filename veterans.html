Veterans Stability & Safety Program

Supporting veterans through housing stability, recovery, safety, and long-term resilience.

Program Overview

The Veterans Stability & Safety Program is a hybrid remote and in-person support model designed for veterans navigating housing instability, alcohol & drug recovery, personal safety challenges, and long-term stability. This program reflects CII’s commitment to infrastructure-driven community benefit and equitable access to essential services across Humboldt County.

Why This Program Matters

Humboldt County’s veterans face unique barriers: rural isolation, limited access to certified AOD programs, transportation challenges, and gaps in housing and recovery support. CII’s program addresses these barriers through accessible, culturally informed, and community-rooted service delivery.

Program Components

  • Remote AOD Stabilization: Structured alcohol & drug recovery support delivered via Zoom or Microsoft Teams.
  • Housing Stability Coaching: Assistance navigating housing insecurity, landlord communication, and resource access.
  • Safety & Crisis Support: Trauma-informed guidance for veterans experiencing personal safety challenges.
  • Case Management: Coordinated support connecting veterans to benefits, healthcare, and community resources.
  • Community Partnerships: Collaboration with tribal nations, local service providers, and veteran organizations.

Our Commitment

CII’s approach is grounded in respect, dignity, and long-term stability. We work closely with tribal governments, rural communities, and local institutions to ensure veterans receive meaningful, accessible, and culturally appropriate support. No poverty wages are tolerated in CII’s staffing model — all program personnel are compensated at or above Humboldt County living wage standards.
